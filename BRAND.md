# Brand guide (Peruvian Universities)

Source: Manual de Identidad 2024.

| Token | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Pantone | Notes |

### Alternative colors (use only when needed)

`#D13B3B`, `#FF914D`, `#FFDE59`, `#00BF63`, `#085678`, `#8C52FF`

### Accent stripe usage

Use the alternative colors in this order for the thin multicolor divider:

`#D13B3B → #FF914D → #FFDE59 → #00BF63 → #05C3DD → #085678 → #8C52FF`

## Typography

1. **Bebas Neue (Bold)**: logo and headers.
2. **Open Sans Condensed (Bold)**: subheaders.
3. **Heebo (Regular)**: body copy.

## Logo usage

1. Primary logo includes the wordmark and three horizontal bars referencing the Peruvian flag.
2. Keep clear space around the logo and do not distort, recolor, or change alignment.
3. Use the primary palette; white or cyan variants are allowed on dark backgrounds.

## Photography style

1. Portraits and group shots of members.
2. Clean, minimal compositions with low depth of field.
3. Emphasize participants during debate activities.
